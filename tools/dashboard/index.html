What the words mean

A
The network being trained right now, playing with the hand-written rules on top (the same rules the installed model uses).
B
A fixed model from 23 Oct, with the same rules. It never changes, so it is the yardstick: if A gets better, A − B goes up.
Check
Every hour A and B each play the same 45 fights against the training bots (same starting situations), on screen in first person.
A − B
How many health points more A comes out ahead in a fight than B does. +3 means A ends each fight 3 points better off than B would. One check is noisy (about ±4), so only several checks in a row mean something.
se (noise)
How far a check's number can be off by chance. A difference smaller than about two se can be luck.
Run
One change to the training, numbered in EXPERIMENTS.md (run 40 = the 40th). Kept or undone by its checks.
Block
A 3-hour piece of the plan with one number and a target; the next block uses a different kind of method if the number did not move.
Punish crit (B1)
Their hit lifts you; you crit them on the way down. "After their crit, next hit a crit" counts, of the times it took a crit and hit back next, how often that hit was a crit.
Jump-in
They jump at you from a few blocks away to crit you on the way down.
K2, the golden rule
Don't knockback-hit a falling player: with ping the knockback arrives late, they still crit you. "K2 broken" counts A's sprint hits on falling opponents (lower is better).
Combo starter
A way to stun them so they can't hit back: deflection T4, reverse deflection T5, make them miss T6, three-block hit T7, hit the rising jumper T8, jump reset T9.
Combo holder
A way to keep hitting once they are stunned: uppercut T10, lunge T11a, hops T11b, hops and lunges T11c, standing crit T2.
The rules
Hand-written behaviour (from the installed model) that presses some keys on top of the network. Most of A's fighting is still decided by them.
PPO
The learning method: the network tries things and the fight result (the reward) makes the better moves likelier.
Copying
Teaching the network to press what a teacher presses. It can only reach the teacher, never pass it; the plan turns it down.
Swings landing
Of its swings, how many hit.
Damage a minute
Damage dealt and taken per minute of fighting in training.
Installed
The model inside your Minecraft (run35-0657 since 5 Nov 21:19).
